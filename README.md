# 📊 Global Superstore – Business Performance Dashboard

A 5-page Power BI dashboard analysing sales, profit, products and customers of a global retail store (Superstore dataset, 2011–2015).

## 🛠️ Tools & Data
- **Tools:** Power BI, DAX measures, Excel
- **Data:** Superstore Dataset 2011–2015 (about 25K orders, 2K customers, 7 markets)

## 🗂️ Pages
**Home** · **Sales Overview** · **Product Insights** · **Customer Insights** · **Conclusion**

## 📈 Key Metrics
| Sales | Profit | Profit Margin | Avg Discount | Orders | Repeat Customers |
|-------|--------|---------------|--------------|--------|------------------|
| 12.64M | 1.47M | 12% | 14% | 25.035K | 88% |

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. Sales grow every year.
2. Technology is the top category (37.5% of sales).
3. APAC is the highest-selling market.
4. Some sub-categories, like Tables, make a loss.

## 💡 Recommendations
1. Reduce discounts on loss-making products.
2. Focus more marketing on APAC.
